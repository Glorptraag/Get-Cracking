The short version: we don't collect anything.

No data collection

Get Cracking has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It does not send any information about you, your child or your device to us or to anyone else.

Photos stay on your device

If you add photo friends, you choose the photos yourself. The app keeps a small copy inside its own storage on your device. They are never uploaded. On iPhone they are included in your own iCloud or computer backup of the device, like any app data. You can remove them in the app at any time. Deleting the app (or clearing this website's data in your browser) removes everything.

Settings and the friend book

Your settings and the friends you have hatched are stored only on your device.

Purchases (iPhone app)

The Whole Nest is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, Apple Account or payment details. The app only checks with Apple, on the device, whether The Whole Nest has been bought.

This website

The web version runs entirely in your browser and can work offline. It uses no cookies. The site is hosted by Vercel, which, like any web host, processes technical request data such as your IP address in order to deliver the pages. We do not use that data to identify or track anyone.

Children

Get Cracking is made for children aged 5 and under, and the iPhone app is in the App Store's Kids Category. Purchases and links out of the app are behind a parental gate. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.
