Get Cracking: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Get Cracking is a wordless visual timer for little ones, for iPhone and on the web. It is made by Glorptraag in Australia.

The short version: we don't collect anything.

What the app does with information

  • No data collection. Get Cracking has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It does not send any information about you, your child or your device to us or to anyone else.
  • Photos stay on your device. If you add photo friends, you choose them yourself (on iPhone with Apple's photo picker). The app keeps a small copy in its own storage on your device and never uploads it. On iPhone, that copy is part of your own iCloud or computer backup of the device, like any app's data. You can remove photos in the app at any time, or delete the app (or clear the website's data) to remove everything.
  • Settings and the friend book are stored only on your device.
  • Purchases (iPhone only) are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, Apple Account or payment details. The app only asks Apple, on the device, whether The Whole Nest has been bought.
  • Children. Get Cracking is made for children aged 5 and under. On iPhone it is in the App Store's Kids Category, and purchases and links sit behind a parental gate. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.
  • The website runs entirely in your browser and can work offline. It uses no cookies. The site is hosted by Vercel, which, like any web host, processes technical request data such as your IP address in order to deliver the pages. We do not use that data to identify or track anyone.
  • Sounds and voices are recordings that ship inside the app. The app never contacts the services they were made with.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
